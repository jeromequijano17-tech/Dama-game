How dama is played
  • Chips move one square diagonally forward. White goes first.
  • Jump an opposing chip to capture it. Men may capture backward too.
  • Captures are required, and you must take the route that captures the most chips.
  • A chip that ends its move on the far row becomes a dama. A dama slides and captures any distance along a diagonal.
  • You lose if you have no legal move, or if your turn timer runs out.
